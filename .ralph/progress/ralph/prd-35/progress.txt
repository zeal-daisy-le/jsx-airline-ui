Issue #15 — Passenger selection step (2026-05-22)

Implemented the full passenger selection booking step at `/booking/passengers`. Created a reusable `PassengerStepper` component (`components/booking/PassengerStepper.tsx`) that renders accessible +/− stepper controls with `role="group"`, `aria-label` on each button, `aria-live="polite"` on the count output, and disabled states at min/max — satisfying WCAG 2.1 AA.
